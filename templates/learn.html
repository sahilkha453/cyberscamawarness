How Online Scammers Trap Victims (4-Stage Anatomy)

Step 1

Initial Contact

Unsolicited SMS, WhatsApp, automated call, or email posing as official bank/utility authority.

Step 2

Creating Panic

Fake threats claiming your bank account, electricity connection, or SIM card will be blocked instantly.

Step 3

Pressure & Trick

Forcing hurried action to share 6-digit OTP, enter UPI PIN, or transfer funds to a fake "safe" account.

Step 4

Financial Loss

Victim shares credentials, resulting in unauthorized debit of savings and personal data compromise.
